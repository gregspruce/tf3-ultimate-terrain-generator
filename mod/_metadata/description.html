Terrain built for transport, and a studio to shape it.

A map-editor window with its own terrain engine, that can also load any vanilla or mod terrain generator. Rivers wind through broad, flat valleys to the sea, mountain ranges have passes a train can climb, and big rivers carry ships while small ones stay easy to bridge.

The Studio

What it does that the game's own generator window cannot:

  • Preview on demand: set everything first, then preview once; the game's window rebuilds after every change. Apply to map always uses the settings you see.
  • A Report before you apply: flat land, rail-grade land, rivers, ship rivers, lakes, pass grades, strait narrows and what to try when a map falls short.
  • Undo puts the previous terrain back.
  • More control than the game's three sliders: twelve map types, five Terrain steps, the edge the sea, big river or strait is on (or no sea), and ten sliders with tooltips.
  • Lower tree density: about half the trees in the same forests, for slower computers.
  • Any generator of your map's climate, the game's own included, runs here too with Preview, Undo and the Report's land and slope numbers to compare them.

The terrain engine

  • Rivers first: the land is shaped around the rivers, so every valley has its river and every river reaches the sea or the map edge.
  • Ship rivers on request: 120-180 m wide and 5 m deep all the way out. Other rivers stay 18-58 m wide, cheap to bridge.
  • Passes a railway can climb on Mountain Range and Alpine: 2.5 % on Gentle up to 6 % on Mountainous, under the game's 8.5 % railway limit (4.0-4.1 % in game on Alpine, for a 4 % target).
  • Room to build: Great River Valley at Hilly had 27 % flat land (under 3 % slope) in game, against 11 % on a game-generated map. Rolling Plains and Delta Lowlands are flatter still.
  • Natural detail: spurs and gullies on the hillsides, smooth plains and valley floors, lakes on the rivers, oxbow lakes, deltas and estuaries.
  • Finished by the game's own rules on Temperate: forests, rock and snow at full 4 m detail.

Map types

  • Great River Valley: a ship river down a broad valley to the sea.
  • Delta Lowlands: the flattest map, the river split into channels.
  • Mountain Range with Passes: one long range, two railway passes.
  • Alpine: snow-capped massifs over hill country, two long passes.
  • Rolling Plains: gentle inland country and a ship river.
  • Lake District: hills with long lakes along the rivers.
  • Large Bays: deep bays where ships cross and trains go round.
  • Peninsulas: short sea routes, long land routes.
  • Island Chain: islands of all sizes, for ships and ferries.
  • Coastal Region: a ragged coast, a coastal plain, hills inland.
  • Highland Plateau: a lowland, an escarpment, a high plateau.
  • Sea Strait: a channel from edge to edge; its narrows (350-400 m) are too wide for the game's automatic town roads, so the big bridges are yours.

How to use

1. Map Editor > Create New Map: Temperate, Small or larger, this mod on the Add Mods tab.

2. Click UTG (top left, next to the map layers button), pick a map type and a Terrain step (Gentle is the easiest), press Preview.

3. Read the Report; try New seed or other settings and Preview again.

4. Tick Keep towns / Keep industries if wanted, then Apply to map. Not happy? Undo.

5. Add towns and industries, save the map and play it from New Game.

Limits

  • Map editor only.
  • Forests, rock and snow on Temperate only; other climates get the terrain shape for now.
  • Undo restores the terrain only, not trees or removed towns and industries.
  • Slower than the game's generator: about 12 s to preview, 3 s to apply on a 14 km map.
  • Few or no rivers on maps under about 6 km.

Guide: what each setting and slider does to the terrain: extended_readme.md in the mod's folder (from mod.io: Users\Public\mod.io\10640\mods\6433560 on the system drive).

Changes in this version

New

  • Two map types: Alpine (separate snow-capped massifs over hill country, with two long passes graded for railways) and Sea Strait (a sea channel splits the map from edge to edge: long sounds for ships and ferries, and a few narrows of about 350-400 m for big bridges; ships always get through). Twelve map types now.
  • No sea where it fits: Sea side None on Mountain Range, Alpine and Highland Plateau, and a Sea box on Great River Valley (the big river leaves the map instead of reaching the sea).
  • Lower tree density check box: about half the tree models for lower-spec computers; forests stay where they are.
  • Tooltips on every Terrain parameter slider, in plain words.

Improved

  • Relief is now called Terrain, Hilly is pre-selected, and flat land now shrinks in even steps from Gentle to Mountainous (the middle steps have much more flat land between the hills than before).
  • Mountain Range passes meet their railway grade at every Terrain step (2.5 % at Gentle up to 6 % at Mountainous), and the rivers behind the range run straight to the far edge.
  • Apply to map makes the preview first when you changed a setting, so it never applies an outdated map.
  • Changing the map type keeps your choices where they still mean the same; Alpine pre-selects Sea side None.
  • The window can be moved again, and generating no longer stalls the game for a tenth of a second at a time.
  • The mod description and the in-mod guide are rewritten: what the Studio and the terrain engine give you, and what each setting does to the map.

Fixed

  • Apply with towns on the map and Keep towns off crashed the game (Undo could too). Fixed.
  • Short ship rivers could run in straight lines with sharp corners; they now bend like the others.
  • With some GUI mods active (mods that replace the game's window component), opening the window could crash the game. The window now always builds on the game's own component.

Planned

  • Forests, rock and snow for Dry, Subarctic and Tropical maps, and map types made for them (Fjord Coast, Mesa Country, Volcanic Islands).
  • Undo that also brings back the trees, and faster generation on the largest maps.
